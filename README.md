# Uloom e Deeniya Hub

Create a website for religious education for mosque. The organization name is "Tauheed Trust" , education organizer named "معھدالعلوم" . You should add address of the place of the class which is "Rafa e aam soceity , Malir Halt , Karachi (near Masjid e Tauheed Rafae aam) The name of the institute is "Uloom e deeniya' "علوم دینیہ" . It include 5 subjects:

1. Tafheem ud din (تفھیم الدین) "Understanding of religion"

2. Usool e Hadith (اصول حدیث) "Rules of Hadith (Saying of Prophet (S))

3. Lughat ul Arabia (لغت العربی) "Dictionary of Arabic"
4. Tajweed ul Qur'an (تجوید القرآن) "Extraction correctly the words of Qur'an"
5. Tarjumat ul Qur'an (ترجمہ القرآن) "Translation of Qur'an"

It has 5 grades name:
1. Grade one (درجہ اولیٰ)
2. Grade two (درجہ ثانی)
3. Grade three (درجہ ثالثہ)
4. Grade four (درجہ الرابع)
5. Grade five (درجہ خامس)

Make different tabs for these grades , then make different tabs for resources , assignments, tests, Recorded lectures, Upcoming Events.
